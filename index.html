📖 How to Play

1 Set Secret Numbers: Each player secretly enters a number (1-100).
2 Take Turns: Players alternate guessing the opponent's secret number.
3 Get Hints: After each guess, you'll get a hint - "Too High" ⬆️ or "Too Low" ⬇️.
4 Win! First player to guess the opponent's number correctly wins! 🏆
5 Draw: If both players use all attempts without guessing correctly, it's a draw! 🤝
💡 Tip: Choose your difficulty wisely! Easy (1-50, 15 tries), Medium (1-100, 10 tries), Hard (1-200, 7 tries).
